Thermal assembly
Section
Method. Lamé thick-cylinder, plane stress, plain cylindrical hub and shaft of equal length. p = δ / (d·(CH/EH + CS/ES)), CH = (Do² + d²)/(Do² − d²) + νH, CS = (d² + Di²)/(d² − Di²) − νS. Capacity: T = μ·p·π·d²·L/2, F = μ·p·π·d·L; combined slip check uses √(F² + (2T/d)²). Operating: δop = δ + d·(αS − αH)·ΔT. Assembly: d·αH·ΔTH − d·αS·ΔTS ≥ δmax + clearance.
Not included. Surface-roughness embedding, centrifugal loosening, end-of-hub pressure peaks and fretting, and the change of E and σy with temperature. Stresses beyond yield are elastic extrapolations; capacity is capped at the first-yield pressure.
